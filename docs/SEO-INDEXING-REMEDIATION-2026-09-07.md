# SEO Indexing Remediation - 2026-09-07

## Evidence before the change

- Google Search Console Page indexing report, last updated 2026-09-04: 117 indexed and 34 not indexed URLs.
- The actionable report group was `Discovered - currently not indexed` with 26 URLs. It contained newly published Blog and News URLs that had not yet been crawled.
- The single `Crawled - currently not indexed` example was an old product URL that now returns a permanent redirect to its canonical category URL. The destination returns `200`, `index, follow`, and its own canonical.
- The production sitemap contained 109 URLs: 13 public pages, 13 product URLs, 6 application URLs, 14 Blog URLs, and 63 News URLs.
- The News inventory contained 74 records. Of 73 published records, 63 were sitemap-eligible and were generated by the `deterministic-editorial-template` pipeline. Many were broad storage-market updates rather than material-specific reference content.

## Change scope

1. Automated News still publishes directly to `/news` when freshness, duplicate and material relevance checks pass.
2. Product matching has a minimum score of `0.55`; a lower environment value cannot weaken it.
3. Only News with a material-specific topic, a score of at least `0.70`, and a related product may enter the sitemap.
4. Existing template-generated News is marked `seo_indexable = false` by migration `20260907-noindex-automated-news.sql`. No content, public URLs, source records or product relationships are deleted.
5. Blog details expose related, real Cowin product/application paths and a BreadcrumbList. The Blog article text and URL are unchanged.

## Google submission

The production cron remains `30 2 */3 * *` UTC. It verifies public URLs, robots and sitemap XML, records the run, and submits the changed sitemap to the Search Console Sitemaps API. API acceptance is a discovery signal, not a guarantee of indexing.
